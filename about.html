About Us

Identity, purpose and values

Introduction

AGMMYS is a youth-led community organisation of the Maheshwari community. Placeholder Establishment and registration details will be added once provided.

Our Vision

To build a connected, progressive and empowered Maheshwari community through unity, participation, shared values and collective development.

Draft messaging, subject to organisational approval.

Our Mission

To encourage youth leadership, strengthen community relationships, preserve cultural heritage and support meaningful initiatives that contribute to the well-being and progress of the community.

Draft messaging, subject to organisational approval.

Our Objectives

Build stronger community connections.

Encourage youth participation.

Preserve community values and traditions.

Promote social and cultural activities.

Support collective growth and development.

Bring community members together.

Our Core Values

Unity

Standing together across families and generations.

Participation

Every member has a part to play.

Respect

For elders, traditions and each other.

Service

Contributing to the community’s well-being.

Importance of Youth Participation

Young members carry the community forward. AGMMYS aims to give them platforms to lead, learn and contribute.

Community Unity and Togetherness

Our emblem of joined hands reflects a simple belief: unity is strength.

Our Commitment to Future Generations

We aim to hand over a community that is connected, informed and proud of its heritage.
